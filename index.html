About Me

AI and Robotics researcher

I'm Elias Kallioras, an engineer and researcher from Athens, and a recent graduate of the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at NTUA. What I find most interesting is interpretability, working out what a trained model actually learns inside, across both language models and robot learning, so that its behaviour can be explained and steered rather than only measured.

This site is mostly for keeping track of what I've done and where I'm going. If something here interests you, feel free to reach out.
